## ⚖️ 2. Legal, Copyright & Safety Audit

1. **Ambient Background Audio**:
   * All background music tracks must be **CC0 (Public Domain)** or commercially licensed royalty-free assets (Pixabay/Freesound).
2. **Piper TTS Neural Voices**:
   * Piper models are licensed under **MIT / Apache 2.0 / Public Domain**. 100% legal for commercial apps on Google Play.
3. **Holy Texts & Philosophical Works**:
   * **Public Domain Translations Only**: KJV Bible, World English Bible, Sahih International Quran, Pickthall Quran, George Long Marcus Aurelius, Swami Swarupananda Gita, Dhammapada.
   * Avoid modern copyright-restricted translations (NIV, ESV) requiring royalties.
