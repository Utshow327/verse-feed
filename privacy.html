Privacy Policy for Verse Feed

Last Updated: July 27, 2026

Welcome to Verse Feed ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled when you use our mobile application and web platform.

1. Information We Collect

Verse Feed is designed to operate with minimal data collection to preserve your privacy and provide a seamless, serverless experience:

  • Google Account Information: If you choose to sign in with Google, we access basic profile details such as your name, email address, and profile picture to personalize your experience.
  • Local Application Data: Your saved verses, bookmarks, religion filters, audio volume, and app preferences are stored locally on your device via standard browser storage.
  • Google Drive AppData Sync: To enable free cross-device synchronization without hosting external servers, Verse Feed requests access to your Google Drive appDataFolder. This is a hidden, application-specific directory inside your personal Google Drive account. Verse Feed only reads and writes its own configuration file (versefeed_data.json) and cannot access, view, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used for the following purposes:

  • To display your saved verses, bookmarks, and audio settings.
  • To synchronize your personal app state across your devices via your Google Drive account when signed in.
  • To authenticate your identity and manage Premium subscription status.

3. Guest Mode & Offline Usage

When you use Verse Feed without signing in or while offline, all app preferences and saved verses remain strictly on your local device storage. No data is transmitted to external servers or cloud accounts during offline usage.

4. Third-Party Services

Verse Feed integrates standard third-party developer platforms:

  • Google Play Services & Billing: Used to authenticate sign-ins and process Google Play Store purchases securely.
  • Piper TTS Engine: Voice synthesis runs locally on your device without transmitting spoken text to remote cloud APIs.

5. Data Security

We do not operate central database servers that store your personal records. Your synchronization files reside inside your secured personal Google account infrastructure. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers or third-party data brokers.

6. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected directly on this page.
