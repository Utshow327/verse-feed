Privacy Policy for Verse Feed

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

Welcome to Verse Feed ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to respecting your privacy and safeguarding your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use our mobile application and web platform.

1. Information We Collect

Verse Feed is built with privacy-by-design and minimal data collection:

  • Account Information: When you create an account or sign in with Google or Email/Password, we collect your email address, display name, and authentication user ID to manage your account identity.
  • User Preferences, Bookmarks & Sync: When signed in, your bookmarked verses, custom folders, religion filters, audio settings, and reading progress history are synchronized across your devices to prevent repetitive verses when switching devices.
  • Guest Mode & Offline Storage: When using the app as a Guest or offline, all preferences and reading history remain strictly on your local device storage.

2. How We Use & Store Data

  • Your account and synchronized bookmarks are stored securely using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore).
  • All network communication is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / SSL encryption.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data to third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertisers.

3. Third-Party Subprocessors

Verse Feed utilizes standard, trusted third-party developer infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure authentication, password recovery, and cross-device database synchronization.
  • Google Play Billing: Used to process optional Premium subscriptions and purchases securely. We never receive or store your financial payment details.
  • Piper TTS Engine: Text-to-speech audio synthesis runs entirely locally on your device using offline WebAssembly. No voice recordings, transcripts, or personal audio data are transmitted to external AI servers.

4. Data Deletion & Account Control (Right to be Forgotten)

You have full control over your personal data:

  • You can permanently delete your account and wipe all synchronized cloud data directly inside the app at any time by navigating to Settings → Profile → Delete Account.
  • Alternatively, you may request permanent data deletion by contacting our support team at utshow.dev@gmail.com.

5. Children's Privacy

Verse Feed does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can immediately delete it.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates and legal compliance standards. Any changes will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions, or data requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at utshow.dev@gmail.com.
